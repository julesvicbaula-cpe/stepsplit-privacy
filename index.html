StepSplit Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Summary

StepSplit is a workout planner and step counter. It works entirely on your phone. It does not collect your personal data, does not send anything to the developer or to any third party, and does not show ads.

What the app stores on your phone

To work, StepSplit saves the following in its private app storage on your device: your weekly workout plan and custom templates, the sets, reps and weights you log, your daily step counts and step history, your step goals, and your reminder settings. This information never leaves your device.

Permissions the app asks for

Physical activity (step counting): used to read your phone's step sensor so the app can show your daily steps and your progress toward your goal. This is optional. If you decline, the rest of the app still works. You can turn step counting off at any time in About > Step counting, or in your phone's settings.

Notifications: used to send workout reminders at the time you choose. This is optional.

Run at startup and vibration: used so that reminders you scheduled are restored after your phone restarts, and for notification and button feedback.

No accounts, ads or tracking

StepSplit has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking. It works fully offline and does not send your data over the internet.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything the app stores by choosing About > Delete all my data, or by uninstalling the app.

Health information

StepSplit is for general fitness tracking and planning. It does not provide medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially if you have a health condition.

Children

StepSplit is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be shown in the app and on this page with a new date.
